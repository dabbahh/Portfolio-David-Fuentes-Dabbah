About me

Hello! My name is David. I am 20 years old, born and raised in Mexico City. I love anything art related from movies to music and even museum pieces, its more than a hobby. Its a way of life to me.

In Mexico I passed the High School IB Diploma where I learned most of my technical writing skills and some artistic as well. Im currently based in Groningen, Netherlands where Im pursuing a Create Media and Game Technologies bachelors degree.

In the 2 years of my program I have focused more on the area of creativity, whether its graphic design, filmmaking, 3D modelling, game design, video editing or digital sculpting. Even some programming.

I believe I am able to bring a new perspective to any project, not only because of my cultural background but because of my passion for creating with the goal of making something that is unique.

Want to see how this translates into real projects? Explore my work.
